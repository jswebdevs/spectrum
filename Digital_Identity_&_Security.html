What It Solves

Three Questions, Answered Every Time

Most identity fraud comes down to three questions. SpectrumID answers all three in a single check.

Is This a Real Person?

Stops fake profiles, stolen identities and people pretending to be someone else, before any account is opened or payment is made.

Is the ID Genuine?

Reads and checks the Smart NID, older NID cards, e-Passports and driving licences automatically. No staff member has to type anything.

Has the Card Been Changed?

Spots swapped photos, edited text, copied holograms and colour printouts made to look like real cards.

How It Works

Three Steps, Under Two Seconds

This is what happens from the moment a customer shows their card to the moment they are approved.

1 Step 1 • Scan the Card

Scan the Card, Skip the Typing

The customer or officer points a phone camera at the card. The app finds the card edges, straightens the image and removes glare by itself.

In about 0.1 seconds, SpectrumID reads the card in Bangla and English and fills in the form:

  • Name in Bangla and English
  • Father's and mother's name
  • Date of birth
  • NID number
  • Gender and nationality
  • Document number and expiry
  • Issuing authority
  • Barcode and passport MRZ data
2 Step 2 • Check the Card

Make Sure the Card Is Genuine

A good colour printout on plastic can fool the eye. SpectrumID looks closer, at three layers a fake rarely gets right:

Fine Print and Line Patterns

Real cards carry tiny text and fine lines that blur when a card is copied or reprinted.

Hologram

A real hologram changes colour as the light moves. A flat print does not.

Chip and Barcode

The data stored in the chip and barcode must match the printed details exactly.

3 Step 3 • Check the Person

Confirm a Real, Live Person

The customer looks at the camera for a moment. They do not need to blink, smile or turn their head. SpectrumID checks that the face has real depth, then matches it with the photo on the card.

Blocks Common Tricks

Printed photos, videos played on a phone or tablet, masks and AI-generated deepfakes.

Under the Hood

How the System Fits Together

Three parts, each with one job. Data is encrypted from the moment it is captured, and it never leaves Bangladesh.

1. Capture

Phones, tablets, POS terminals, fingerprint scanners and kiosks collect the card image, face and fingerprints.

2. SpectrumID Engine

Runs on your own servers or a local data centre. It reads cards, checks for forgery, tests liveness and matches faces and fingerprints.

3. Secure Vault

Keeps encryption keys in a hardware security module (HSM) and records every check in an audit log that cannot be altered.
