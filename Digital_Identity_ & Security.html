Step-by-Step Engine Breakdown

The 3-Step Automated Verification Lifecycle

From raw camera ingestion to cryptographic authorization in under 2 seconds. Every verification executes across three forensic defense rings.

1 Step 01 • Document Ingestion

Smart Document Capture & 0.1s AI-OCR Auto-Fill

Our client capture SDK utilizes real-time optical frame guidance to automatically detect card edges, correct skew, and eliminate laminate flash glare.

In just 0.1 seconds, our proprietary neural OCR engine extracts 22+ discrete citizen fields across dual scripts without human touch:

  • Name & Bengali Name (‌নাম‌)
  • Father & Mother Name
  • Date of Birth (DoB)
  • Personal ID / NID Number
  • Nationality & Gender
  • Document Number & Expiry
  • Issuing Country & Authority
  • 2D Barcode & MRZ Decryption
2 Step 02 • Forensic Authentication

Multi-Layer Forensic & Hologram Security

Counterfeit IDs often use high-resolution color printing on PVC. SpectrumID inspects the physical artifact across three deep forensic layers to guarantee physical authenticity:

Microprint & Guilloche Pattern Validation

Analyzes fine security vector lines and micro-lettering that blur when re-scanned or photocopied.

Holographic & Optical Reflectance Check

Measures dynamic light diffraction across national security holograms to spot flat paper prints.

Cryptographic Smart Chip Cross-Check

Verifies data consistency between physical laser engraving, 2D PDF417 barcodes, and embedded smart chips.

3 Step 03 • Biometric Verification

Multimodal Biometrics & 3D Passive Liveness

SpectrumID provides certified iBeta Level 1 & 2 compliant 3D passive liveness detection. Users don't need to blink or turn their heads—our models detect sub-millimeter depth contours in a fraction of a second.

100% Anti-Spoof Interception
