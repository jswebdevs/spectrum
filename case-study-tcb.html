TCB Smart Family Card

Updated 9 November 2026

TCB sells essential goods at low prices to families on low incomes across Bangladesh. Spectrum built the system that registers these families, gives each one a smart family card, and lets dealers check the card before handing over the goods.

Client
Trading Corporation of Bangladesh (TCB), Ministry of Commerce
Sector
Social safety net and food subsidy
What we built
Family registration, smart family cards and the TCB Card Sheba app
Live system
digital.tcbsheba.com
Mobile apps
Google Play

The problem

TCB supports more than 10 million families in all 64 districts. Before the smart card, distribution relied on paper coupons and manual lists.

Paper coupons were easy to copy, the same family could appear on a list more than once, and TCB had no live view of how much had been handed out, where, and to whom.

What we built

A national system that ties every card to a real, registered family:

  • Family registration checked against the NID, so each family is registered only once.
  • A smart family card for every family, with an encrypted QR code that cannot simply be photocopied.
  • The TCB Card Sheba Android app, which dealers and authorised staff use to scan and verify each card before a sale and to activate new cards. Beneficiaries can now activate their own cards in the app too.
  • A live view for TCB of stock and sales at more than 10,000 licensed dealers.

How it works

Register

Each family is registered with its NID. Duplicate registrations are flagged straight away.

Issue the card

The family receives a smart family card with a secure QR code.

Check at the dealer

The dealer scans the card in the TCB Card Sheba app before handing over the goods.

Track every sale

Each sale is recorded, so TCB can see stock and distribution as it happens.

Results

  • 10M+Families registered
  • 57M+People who benefit
  • 10,000+Dealers using the app
  • 100K+TCB Card Sheba app downloads
  • 99.8%Fewer duplicate claims
  • More than 10 million families registered in one database without duplicates.
  • Over 57 million people benefit from distribution that is open and hard to cheat.
  • Duplicate claims cut by 99.8%, because every sale is checked against the family record.
  • The TCB Card Sheba app has been downloaded more than 100,000 times from Google Play.

The technology, in plain words

  • NID-based duplicate check: compares each new family with everyone already registered.
  • Encrypted QR cards: the code can be read by the dealer app but not forged.
  • Offline-ready dealer app (TCB Card Sheba): records sales without internet and sends them when the connection returns.
  • Cloud system built for heavy use: handles busy distribution days across the country.
