Novotheatre eTicket

Updated 9 November 2026

Novotheatre welcomes visitors to space shows, 5D movies, virtual reality and science exhibits. Spectrum built its online ticketing: a web portal and the Novotheatre eTicket app, where visitors pick a date and a show, buy a ticket and enter with a barcode.

The problem

Without online ticketing, visitors, school groups and families have to queue at the counter to buy tickets, without knowing in advance what is showing and when.

Counter-only sales also make it harder to plan for busy days and to keep every sale in one record.

What we built

Online ticketing on the web and on Android:

  • An online ticket portal where visitors choose a date and see each show with its time, duration and price.
  • The Novotheatre eTicket Android app, in Bangla, with log in and “buy ticket online” on the home screen.
  • Shows and exhibits in one place, such as the entrance ticket, 5D movies, VR and the digital and science exhibits.
  • Barcode tickets that are checked at the entry gates.
  • Sales records kept in one system.

How it works

Pick a date

Choose a show

Each show lists its time, duration and price.

Buy the ticket

The visitor buys online and gets a ticket with a barcode.

Enter at the gate

The barcode is checked at the gate on arrival.

The technology, in plain words

  • Ticket portal: shows by date, with prices and a buy button.
  • Android app: the same tickets on a phone, in Bangla.
  • Barcode tickets: each ticket carries a barcode for the entry gate.
  • One sales record: online and app sales in the same system.
