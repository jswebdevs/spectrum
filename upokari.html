Upokari Verification Engine

Catch Forged Documents in Seconds

The engine at upokari.ai checks the documents people are most often cheated with: land records, TCB cards and National IDs. It catches counterfeit cards, photos of screens, photocopies and edited genuine originals, then checks the details against the issuing record. No paperwork and no office visit.

Each check ends with a clear verdict: Genuine, Counterfeit, Replay (a photo of a screen), Copy or Spoof.

Six checks on every card

Counterfeit cards

Fonts, layout, fine patterns, microprint, hologram and MRZ checksum compared with the genuine template.

Photos of screens

Spots cards captured from a phone or monitor screen by the tell-tale patterns and glare they leave.

Photocopies and scans

Finds the toner dots, soft edges and lost detail that give away a copy.

Edited genuine cards

Catches a real card with a swapped photo, laid-over details or a laminated edit.

Holograms

Checks holograms and colour-shifting features across angles.

Registry check

Matches the card number, holder details and seal with the issuing registry before giving a verdict.

How land records are sealed: seven steps

From fragile registry paper to a certified copy that anyone can check.

Step 1

Archival scan

Registry paper is scanned at 300 to 600 DPI on scanners suited to bound or fragile records.

Step 2

Reading the fields

Software tuned for Bangla and English reads the Mouza, Khatian and Dag numbers, owner names, land area and dates.

Step 3

Officer review

A registry officer checks the reading side by side with the scan. Nothing is approved automatically.

Step 4

Sealing

Each page gets a digital fingerprint (SHA-256), chained page to page into one fingerprint for the whole document.

Step 5

Watermark and QR

A watermark and a QR code on every page carry the document ID, page number and page fingerprint.

Step 6

Stamp and copy code

A unique ID and an anti-copy micro-pattern are printed over the official stamp on the last page.

Step 7

Issue and verify

The certified Nokol is printed from the sealed master. Anyone can scan it or upload a photo for a Genuine, Tampered or Copy verdict.

Built so no one can quietly change a record

  • Page-by-page fingerprints: every page’s SHA-256 fingerprint is chained to the next, so changing one page breaks the chain.
  • Anchored on a private blockchain: document fingerprints are recorded on Hyperledger Fabric, so even a database administrator cannot alter them unnoticed. What is a blockchain?
  • Keys in secure hardware: the signing keys are sealed in a FIPS 140-3 hardware security module.
  • People stay in charge: a registry officer approves every reading before a record is sealed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is upokari.ai the same as the Upokari app?

They are part of the same family but do different jobs. upokari.ai is the verification engine that checks whether a document is genuine. The Upokari app is a secure mailbox and document vault on your phone. Upokari eCom is an online marketplace with its own seller app.

Which documents can the Verification Engine check?

Land documents (Nokol, Khatian, Dag and plot sheets, CS and RS records, mutation and deed copies), TCB cards and National ID cards are live now. e-Passports and travel documents, and licences and certificates such as driving and trade licences, are rolling out.

How does it tell a fake from a real card?

It compares fonts, layout, fine background patterns, microprint, holograms and the MRZ checksum with the genuine template, looks for signs of a photo of a screen or a photocopy, and then matches the card details with the issuing registry before giving a verdict.

Is the Upokari app available in Bangla?

Yes. You can switch between Bangla and English anywhere in the app.

How is my information protected in the app?

Documents are kept in an encrypted vault with access control, and you can lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or a PIN.

Can our organisation send notices or invoices through Upokari?

Upokari is built as a platform shared by many organisations. Approved organisations connect as senders and deliver digitally signed, QR-coded notices and invoices to people’s wallets. Contact us to discuss connecting your organisation.
