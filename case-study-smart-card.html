Smart Card Issuance

Updated 9 November 2026

Driving licences and transport cards must be hard to fake and quick to produce. Spectrum built the system that prints, encodes and issues secure smart cards in large numbers.

Client
National transport and licensing authorities
Sector
Secure ID and licence cards
What we built
A card personalisation and issuance system
Live system
Runs inside the client's secure network

The problem

The authorities needed to issue a very large number of cards, such as driving licences and transport cards, quickly and without mistakes.

Each card had to be very hard to copy. That meant writing secret keys onto each chip safely and adding security features that show any tampering.

What we built

One central system that controls every card from order to delivery:

  • A card management system (CPMS) that tracks each card from the data entry to the finished card.
  • Connected laser engraving machines and chip programmers, so printing and encoding happen in one flow.
  • Hardware security modules (HSMs) that keep the chip keys locked away, even from operators.
  • Fingerprint on the card, so the card can confirm its holder by matching the finger on the card itself.
  • Handheld scanners that let officers check a card on the spot, even without internet.

How it works

Enrol

The holder's details and fingerprint are captured and checked.

Personalise

The card is laser engraved and its chip is encoded in one run.

Secure

Secret keys are written from the HSM, so they are never exposed.

Check in the field

Officers scan the card with a handheld reader, online or offline.

Results

  • 15,000+Cards a day
  • 0Counterfeit cases reported
  • OfflineChecks in the field
  • More than 15,000 smart cards personalised every day.
  • No counterfeit incidents reported on the issued chip cards.
  • Officers can check a card instantly with a handheld scanner, even offline.

The technology, in plain words

  • Contact and contactless chips: works with both kinds of card chip (ISO 7816 and ISO 14443).
  • Laser engraving: burns details into the card so they cannot be scraped off and reprinted.
  • Hardware security modules: special devices that store the secret keys safely.
  • Match-on-card: the fingerprint check happens on the card, so the fingerprint never leaves it.
