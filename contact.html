Common Questions

Do you work with private companies, or only with government?

Both. We work with government offices, banks, telecom operators and private companies, on anything from a single system to a national programme.

Can we see a live demo?

Yes. Say what you would like to see in your message, and we will arrange a demo, in person or online.

Do you respond to tenders and RFPs?

Yes. Send us the tender details or documents, choose "Tender or RFP" in the form, and our team will get back to you.

What should I write in my message?

A few lines are enough: what you want to do, roughly how many people or records it covers, and when you need it. We will ask about the rest on the call.
