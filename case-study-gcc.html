Gazipur City Corporation e-Revenue

Updated 9 November 2026

Gazipur City Corporation collects holding tax and issues trade licences for one of Bangladesh’s largest industrial cities. Spectrum built its e-Revenue system: an online service portal and the My GCC app, where citizens and businesses check, calculate, renew and keep track of what they owe.

The problem

Holding tax and trade licences are a city corporation’s main sources of revenue, and they touch thousands of homes, shops and factories.

Without an online system, people have to visit the office to find out what they owe, to renew a licence or to check an application, and the city has no quick view of assessments, payments and dues across its zones.

What we built

One e-Revenue system on the web and on Android:

  • An online service portal in Bangla, where citizens see their application information, check and verify details for trade licences and holding tax, renew services, and read notices.
  • Holding tax and trade licence online management, with a login for citizens and businesses.
  • A holding tax calculator that works out the tax from the building’s use (own, rented or partly rented), its type (industrial, commercial, residential or government) and its tax zone.
  • All tax zones covered: Tongi, Pubail, Gacha, Joydebpur, Kaultia, Basan, Konabari and Kashimpur, plus the BSCIC industrial areas in Tongi and Konabari.
  • A fee calculator for every citizen service the city corporation provides.
  • The My GCC Android app, with registration using NID and mobile number, OTP verification, and a dashboard of tax amounts, assessments and dues by year, and trade licences by zone.
  • One front door that also links to the city’s water connection and complaint portal and its market lease and shop rent system.

How it works

Register

The citizen or business registers with their NID and mobile number and confirms with an OTP.

Check and calculate

They check their holding or licence details and see the tax or fee due.

Apply or renew

They apply for or renew a trade licence or other service online.

Track it

They follow their application, and the city sees assessments and dues on dashboards.

Results

  • 10Tax zones and industrial areas
  • 2Main revenues online: holding tax and trade licences
  • Web + appPortal and My GCC
  • Citizens and businesses can check what they owe and renew services without visiting the office.
  • Holding tax is calculated the same way for everyone, by building use, type and zone.
  • The city corporation sees tax, assessments and dues by year and trade licences by zone on its dashboards.
  • Water, market rent, holding tax and trade licence services are reachable from one portal.

The technology, in plain words

  • Bangla-first portal: every screen in Bangla for the city’s residents.
  • Rule-based tax calculator: the same rules for every holding, by use, type and zone.
  • NID and OTP sign-up: confirms who is registering before they see their records.
  • Dashboards: charts of tax, assessments and dues by year, and licences by zone.
