Our Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Spectrum IT Solutions Ltd. (“Spectrum”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information on this website, spectrum.com.bd. We collect as little as we can, only to reply to you and to run the site, and we never sell your information.

The short version

  • We only collect what you type into our contact form, so we can reply to you.
  • This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • A few trusted services help run the site: Formspree delivers form messages, and Google provides the fonts and the map on the Contact page.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.

Who we are

Spectrum IT Solutions Ltd. is a software company registered in Bangladesh. We are responsible for the personal information collected through this website. You can reach us using the details at the end of this page.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you send a message through our contact form, we receive:

  • Your name and, if you add it, your organisation.
  • Your email address and/or phone number, so we can reply.
  • The topic you choose, and the product name if you came from an “Ask for a quote” button.
  • Your message, and anything else you choose to tell us in it.

Please do not include sensitive information, such as NID numbers, bank details or health information, in the form. We do not need it to reply.

Information collected automatically

Like every website, our web server and the services below receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. This is needed to show you the page and to keep the site secure. We do not use it to identify you or build a profile of you.

How we use it

We use the information you send us only to:

  • Reply to your question or request, and follow up on it.
  • Prepare a quote, proposal or demo you asked for.
  • Keep a record of our conversation with you, so we can help you better next time.
  • Keep the website working and secure.

We do not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing unless you ask us to.

Why we are allowed to use it

We use your information because you asked us to contact you, or because it is needed to take steps towards a contract you are interested in. For the basic technical information, we rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure website. We follow the applicable laws of Bangladesh on data protection.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run this website, and only what they need:

  • Formspree receives the contact form and forwards it to our email. Formspree is based in the United States and handles the data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering used on every page. Your browser asks Google for the font files, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • Google Maps shows our office location on the Contact page. The map is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies when you view or use it.
  • Our email and hosting providers, which store our emails and the website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid request from a court or a government authority. We never sell your information.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The Google Maps map on the Contact page may set cookies from Google when it loads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form messages for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any work that follows, and then for up to two years so we have a record of our conversation. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as our contract and the law require. You can ask us to delete your messages sooner.

How we protect it

We follow ISO 27001 practices for information security. The website is served over a secure connection (HTTPS), and only the staff who need to reply to you can read your messages. No system is completely secure, so please do not send us sensitive information through the form.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your information, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Stop contacting you.

Email contact@spectrum.com.bd with your request. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act, and we will reply within 30 days.

Our apps and client systems

This policy covers only this website. Our mobile apps, such as Upokari, TCB Card Sheba and Officers’ Club Dhaka, and the systems we build for clients each have their own privacy policy, published in the app store listing or by the organisation that runs the system. Where we run a system for a client, that client decides how personal information is used, and we follow their instructions.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as client portals, app stores and social media. We are not responsible for how those websites handle your information. Please read their privacy policies.

Children

This website is meant for organisations and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make an important change, we will say so on this page.
