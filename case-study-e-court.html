Industrial e-Court

Updated 9 November 2026

Workers and employers take their disputes to Bangladesh's labour courts. Spectrum built the Industrial e-Court system so these cases can be filed, scheduled and followed online instead of through paper files.

Client
Ministry of Labour and Employment and the Labour Courts of Bangladesh
Sector
Courts and justice
What we built
An online case management system for the labour courts
Live system
ecourt.gov.bd

The problem

The labour courts ran on paper. Case files were kept in physical dockets, hearings were scheduled by hand, and summons were delivered in person, which was slow.

Factory workers often had no way to check their next hearing date without travelling to the court.

What we built

One online system for the whole life of a case:

  • Online case filing, so a case can be started without paper forms.
  • The daily cause list published online automatically, showing which cases are heard and when.
  • Digital case files on the judge's bench, instead of folders passed around the courtroom.
  • A case log that shows every change, so records cannot be quietly altered.
  • SMS alerts to tell the people involved about hearing dates and orders.

How it works

File the case

The case is filed online with its documents.

Schedule

Hearings are scheduled and the cause list is published online every day.

Notify

Parties get SMS alerts about their hearing dates and orders.

Hear and record

The judge works from the digital file, and every order is saved to the case record.

Results

  • 85%Less delay in scheduling
  • 100%Paperless case tracking
  • SMSAlerts for hearing dates
  • Scheduling delays cut by 85%, because the cause list is published online straight away.
  • Cases can be tracked without paper by workers, employers and lawyers across all labour court benches.
  • Hearing notices and orders are sent digitally, with a record of who did what and when.

The technology, in plain words

  • Case workflow engine: moves each case through filing, hearing and judgment in the right order.
  • Digital signatures (PKI): show who approved each order and that it has not been changed.
  • Automatic cause lists: built from the case records every day.
  • SMS gateway: sends hearing alerts to the people involved.
