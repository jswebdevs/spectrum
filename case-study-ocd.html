Officers’ Club Dhaka

Updated 9 November 2026

Officers’ Club Dhaka, on Baily Road, has served government officers since 1967 and has about 6,600 general members. Spectrum built its website, member portal and Android and iOS apps, where members can check availability and book the club’s halls, guest house rooms and salon online.

The problem

A club with thousands of members needs one place to share news and to manage bookings for its halls, guest house and services.

Without an online system, members have to phone or visit the club office to find out whether a date is free, then book and pay in person, and staff have to keep track of every request by hand.

What we built

A website and two mobile apps for members, with the club’s bookings built in:

  • The club website, with information about the club, its committees, and facilities such as the swimming pool, gym, library, cafeteria, catering and laundry.
  • A member login, so members can use the services meant for them.
  • Hall booking with a reservation calendar, an online booking form, a booking status check and online payment.
  • Guest house booking that works the same way, from checking free dates to paying.
  • Salon booking and payment online.
  • A notice board on the home page for club notices, and a feedback page.
  • Android and iOS apps, published on Google Play and the App Store, where members and guests can explore the club’s facilities, see notices and events, book halls, the guest house and the salon, and sign in to the member portal.
  • Dues and payments in the app: members see what they owe for the club, the cafeteria and hall bookings, and pay with one tap.
  • Food ordering from the cafeteria menus, with a cart, order summary and checkout.

How it works

Check the calendar

The member sees which dates are already reserved for each hall or guest room.

Book online

They choose a free date and send the booking request online.

Pay online

The booking is paid for online, as part of the same process.

Check the status

Members can look up their booking status at any time, without calling the club.

The technology, in plain words

  • Booking calendars: show reserved and free dates for each hall and guest room.
  • Online payment: members pay for a booking in the same flow as making it.
  • Member login: keeps member services separate from the public pages.
  • Notice board: the club posts notices that members see on the home page.
  • Android and iOS apps: the same club services on members’ phones, published by Spectrum on Google Play and the App Store.
