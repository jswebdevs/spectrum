National e-Licensing

Updated 9 November 2026

Getting a trade licence or an import-export permit used to mean paper forms, office visits and weeks of waiting. Spectrum built one online portal where businesses apply, pay and receive their licence.

Client
Government regulatory authorities and ministries
Sector
Government licensing and permits
What we built
A single-window portal for licences and permits
Live system
licence.gov.bd

The problem

Applications were made on paper and checked in person. Each one passed from desk to desk, and it could take weeks to get a decision.

Applicants could not see where their application was, and payments were hard to track.

What we built

A single window for the whole licence process:

  • Online applications with document uploads, so there is no need to visit an office.
  • Automatic routing sends each application to the right officer, based on clear rules.
  • Approval with an electronic signature, recorded in the system.
  • Online fee payment, connected to Bangladesh Bank's e-challan.
  • Digitally signed licences with a QR code that anyone can scan to check.

How it works

Apply

The business fills in the form online and uploads its documents.

Review

The application goes to the right officer automatically, and its status is visible.

Pay

The fee is paid online through e-challan.

Receive and verify

The signed licence is issued with a QR code that anyone can check.

Results

  • 48 hoursDown from 21 days
  • 100,000+Licences issued
  • QROn every licence
  • The time to get a licence fell from 21 days to under 48 hours.
  • More than 100,000 licences issued, each one checkable by QR code.
  • Fees collected directly through Bangladesh Bank's e-challan.

The technology, in plain words

  • Single-window portal: one place to apply, pay, follow progress and download the licence.
  • Rule-based routing: sends each application to the right desk without manual sorting.
  • Digital signatures (PKI): prove which office issued a licence and that it has not been changed.
  • e-Challan link: connects payments to Bangladesh Bank.
